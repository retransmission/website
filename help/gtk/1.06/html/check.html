How do I manually recheck my files?

If Retransmission crashes, you may want to manually recheck your files:

  • to verify that the download has not been corrupted
  • to ensure Retransmission continues where it left off and doesn't start over

To do this:

  1. Select the relevant torrent.
  2. Go to the Torrent menu >> Verify Local Data.
